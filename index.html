HOW WE PLAY TOGETHER

Guild rules.

Read in Discord ↗

Welcome to Exalted! We’re here to enjoy AION 2, improve together, and build a guild people want to log in for.

  1. Respect everyone

    No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or personal attacks. Friendly banter should be fun for everyone involved.

  2. Keep disagreements constructive

    Discuss problems calmly. Take disputes to an officer instead of starting arguments in public channels.

  3. Play fairly

    No cheating, botting, scams, or deliberate abuse of exploits.

  4. Respect group commitments

    Arrive prepared for scheduled runs. If you’ll be late or can’t attend, let the group know. Real life comes first—communication matters.

  5. Follow the run leader

    During organized activities, keep voice chat clear when calls are being made. Offer feedback respectfully.

  6. Agree on loot rules first

    Follow the loot arrangements announced before a run. Ask if anything is unclear, and bring disputes to an officer.

  7. Help members learn

    Give useful advice without insulting someone’s class, gear, damage, or experience. Everyone starts somewhere.

  8. Use the right channels

    Keep posts relevant. No spam, unsolicited advertising, explicit content, or sharing someone’s private information.

  9. Set up your guild profile

    Choose your classes, party roles, and optional alerts in #role-requests. Follow START HERE to link your own character.

  10. Represent Exalted well

    Compete hard and treat allies, opponents, and other guilds with respect.

Enforcement. Officers may issue warnings, timeouts, removal from activities, kicks, or bans depending on the situation. Serious violations may result in immediate removal. Contact an officer privately with concerns or appeals.
